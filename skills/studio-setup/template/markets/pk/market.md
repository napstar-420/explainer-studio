# Market pack: Pakistan

The market the reference videos were made for: Pakistani viewers, local brands (sweets, snacks,
fintech, telecoms), narration originally in Urdu. Use when making videos for a Pakistani
audience in English (or adapt the voice settings for Urdu).

- Language: clear international English; prices in Rs; local terms where natural (desi,
  kiryana/general store).
- Voice: calm male narrator, warm storyteller, ~135–155 wpm (`markets/pk/voice.json`).
- Settings: Karachi/Lahore streets, colonial-era buildings, general stores with jars,
  rooftops with kids, bakeries, rickshaws; characters in shalwar kameez, waistcoats, caps,
  school uniforms (see the reference contact sheets).
- Sources: Dawn, The Express Tribune, Business Recorder, The News, Profit (Pakistan Today),
  SECP filings, company sites.
- Legal care: attribute causes and estimates; rumours only as attributed rumours; generic
  figures for real people; don't copy logos.
- Topic examples (from the references): BP Spacer, Oye Hoye, JazzCash.

## Full script (English)

Today, millions of people in Pakistan use JazzCash to send money, pay bills and make online payments. But did you know it started out as a service called Mobicash? The story began in 2012, when Mobilink teamed up with Waseela Microfinance Bank to launch Mobicash. Back then, to send money or pay a bill, you often had to go to a bank, where even a small task took a lot of time. Mobicash made this easy. People could send money, receive it, and pay utility bills through their nearby shopkeeper. They could also send money and do other financial tasks from their mobile phone. At first, the service went by Mobilink's name. But in 2016, Mobilink introduced its new identity, Jazz, so Mobicash became JazzCash. After that, JazzCash kept improving its mobile app, online payments and business services. In 2020, it also launched a separate app for businesses, so shopkeepers could accept digital payments. Today, JazzCash isn't just a way to send money; it's one of Pakistan's biggest digital financial platforms.
